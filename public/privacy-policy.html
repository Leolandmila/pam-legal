Privacy Policy for PAM (Parent Admin Manager)

Last Updated: November 16, 2025

Introduction

Parent Admin Manager ("PAM," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users and complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information from parents and their children.

This app is designed for parents and legal guardians. Children under 13 should only use this app under parental supervision, and we require verifiable parental consent before collecting any information from children.

---

Information We Collect

1. Parent/Guardian Information

When you register for PAM, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Account creation date

2. Child Information (With Parental Consent)

With your explicit consent, we collect and store information about your child(ren):

Basic Information:
  • Child's first name
  • Date of birth
  • Gender (optional)
  • Photos (optional)
  • State of residence (for localized content)
Development Data:
  • Developmental milestones achieved
  • Growth measurements (height, weight, head circumference)
  • Health tracking (feeding schedules, sleep patterns, diaper changes)
  • Custom milestone tracking
Why We Collect This Information:

This information is used solely to help you track and monitor your child's development and provide personalized milestone recommendations.

3. Usage Information

We automatically collect:

  • App usage statistics
  • Device information (type, OS version)
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Feature usage analytics

---

How We Use Your Information

For Parents:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your identity
  • Send important notifications about your account
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our services

For Children (With Your Consent):

  • Display developmental milestones appropriate for your child's age
  • Track and visualize your child's growth and development
  • Provide state-specific resources and information
  • Generate personalized milestone recommendations
  • Allow you to share progress with family members (if you choose)

---

COPPA Compliance

Parental Consent

We comply with COPPA by:

1. Age Verification: We verify that users are 13 years or older, or have parental consent

2. Parental Notice: We provide clear notice of what information we collect from children

3. Parental Consent: We obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting children's information

4. Parental Access: Parents can review, modify, or delete their child's information at any time

5. Data Minimization: We only collect information necessary for the app's functionality

Your Rights as a Parent

You have the right to:

  • Review your child's personal information
  • Delete your child's information at any time
  • Refuse further collection or use of your child's information
  • Request a copy of all data we have about your child
To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@parentaladminmanager.com

---

Data Storage and Security

Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Firebase Security: Data stored on Google Firebase with enterprise-grade security
  • Access Controls: Strict authentication and authorization controls
  • Biometric Protection: Optional Face ID/Touch ID for app access
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and penetration testing

Data Location

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers in the United States. Firebase complies with:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
  • SOC 2/SOC 3 certifications

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings.

When you delete your account:

  • All child data is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Backup copies are purged within 90 days
  • Aggregated, anonymized data may be retained for analytics

---

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We DO NOT:

  • ❌ Sell your child's information to third parties
  • ❌ Share your child's information for advertising purposes
  • ❌ Use your child's information for marketing
  • ❌ Allow third-party tracking of children

We MAY Share Information:

Service Providers:
  • Google Firebase (data storage and authentication)
  • Sentry (error tracking - with PII removed)
Legal Requirements:
  • If required by law or legal process
  • To protect the safety of our users
  • To enforce our Terms of Service
With Your Consent:
  • Family sharing features (only with your explicit permission)
  • Healthcare providers (only if you choose to share reports)

---

Third-Party Services

PAM uses the following third-party services:

Google Firebase

  • Purpose: Data storage, authentication, analytics
  • Data Shared: Account information, child data (with consent)
  • Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Sentry

  • Purpose: Error tracking and crash reporting
  • Data Shared: Device info, app version, error logs (NO PII, NO screenshots)
  • Privacy Policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

We have configured these services to comply with COPPA and minimize data collection from children.

---

Children Under 13

PAM is designed for parents to track their children's development. While the app collects information about children under 13, this collection:

  • Only occurs with verifiable parental consent
  • Is limited to information necessary for milestone tracking
  • Can be reviewed and deleted by parents at any time
  • Is not used for advertising or shared with third parties

If we discover we have collected information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.

Your Privacy Rights

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-out of the "sale" of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

European Residents (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have additional rights:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

All Users

Regardless of location, you can:

  • Access your data through the app
  • Export your data in JSON format
  • Delete your account and all data
  • Contact us with privacy questions

---

Data Access and Deletion

In-App Controls

You can manage your data directly in the app:

  • Settings → Privacy → View My Data - See all collected information
  • Settings → Privacy → Export Data - Download a copy of your data
  • Settings → Account → Delete Account - Permanently delete all data

Email Requests

For assistance with data access or deletion:

  • Email: privacy@parentaladminmanager.com
  • Response time: Within 30 days
  • Verification required for security
